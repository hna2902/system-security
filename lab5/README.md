## Noi dung da thuc hien
- Thiet lap 3 vung mang (LAN, WAN, DMZ) su dung pfSense, Windows Server 2022, Windows 10 va Kali Linux trong moi truong VMware
- Cau hinh IP tinh, NAT (Network Address Translation) va giao dien web quan tri cho pfSense
- Xay dung cac quy tac tuong lua (Firewall Rules) dua tren nguyen tac Top-Down de kiem soat luu luong mang
- Thuc hien Tinh huong 1: Loc giao thuc mang (Chan ICMP, cho phep cac dich vu cot loi HTTP/HTTPS va DNS)
- Thuc hien Tinh huong 2: Kiem soat truy cap theo IP (Chi cap phep Internet cho 1 may host cu the, block toan bo phan con lai cua mang LAN)
- Thuc hien Tinh huong 3: Bao mat vung DMZ (Thiet lap rule co lap, khong cho phep vung DMZ ket noi nguoc vao mang noi bo LAN)
- Thuc hien Reset States bang cong cu Diagnostics de ap dung rule va kiem thu ket qua bang lenh ping/trinh duyet
